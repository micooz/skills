# Voice patterns

Derived from 92 published posts (2023–2026). The numbers describe a body of work; they are not quotas for every new piece.

## Corpus snapshot

- Median length: about 1,800 Chinese characters. 75% are under ~2,900; technical deep dives reach 5,000–7,000.
- Median paragraph: roughly 30–45 Chinese characters. The 90th percentile is ~75–85. Long paragraphs are the exception.
- Median title: 16 Chinese characters. Most titles are 10–20.
- Headings: almost all are `#` or `##`; there are effectively no deep heading levels.
- First person is dominant: "我" appears about 57 times per 10,000 characters, "我们" about 23, "你" about 18.
- Questions are normal: about two question marks per article.
- Contrast words are common: "但是", "不过", "其实". "换句话说" and "说白了" are essentially absent.
- Bold is not universal. It marks a load-bearing claim in some pieces; many video transcripts use no bold at all.
- Lists are format-specific. Technical explainers and list posts use them; many personal essays use none.

## Moves that recur

### Start with the occasion

Name the moment, not the category:

> 前阵子我老在跟 AI 聊天。聊着聊着，就会留下一些“回头再说”的事。

### Ask the reader directly

Questions are used to pivot, not to fill space:

> 你是一个“内卷”的人还是一个“躺平”的人？

> 你有没有想过，这个时间到底是怎么来的？

### Turn with "但 / 不过 / 其实"

A common shape: state the obvious view, then turn it.

> 这句话对也不对。

> 听起来挺合理，不过真实情况往往更复杂。

Do not turn every paragraph. The turn lands because the previous statement was allowed to stand.

### Use a personal number or detail

Specific facts make the piece sound lived-in:

> 这个项目我断断续续做了两周，最后能跑起来的时候，还是有点意外。

> 2016 年毕业前夕，我去成都天府软件园面了一次阿里。

### Admit the hook

A self-aware opening is allowed:

> 感谢你点进来，这个标题看着挺唬人。

Then deliver the real point. Do not use this as a substitute for one.

### Let an aside carry tone

> 幸好是个人项目，否则在严肃的公司流程里，第二天可能就得走人了（笑）。

One or two per piece is enough.

### Make the judgment plain

> 设计难，做起来不难。

> 我不想再为一个小功能维护一整套抽象。

The sentence can be blunt. It does not need a metaphor to qualify as writing.

### Use an earthy metaphor

Metaphors work when they are concrete:

> 给氛围编程泼一盆冰水。

> 地基不稳，地动山摇。

Avoid metaphors that only sound literary. If the image does not clarify the point, cut it.

### Label sections plainly

Headings are labels, not copy:

> 好奇心

> 传统 UI 范式

> 如何应变？

Do not write a clever mini-headline for every section.

### End with a question, a personal line, or a practical link

Personal essays often end with an invitation:

> 你觉得这个回答如何？欢迎交流。

Technical pieces can end with a short summary. Product notes often end with the link.

## Format notes

- Video companion: spoken narration; images carry steps; short paragraphs; occasional "Emm…", "没错", "误".
- Technical explainer: one concept per section; concrete examples; a short summary is allowed.
- Opinion piece: strong claim up front; sections defend it; avoid a summary that only repeats.
- List / Q&A: numbered points and plain subheadings; keep each item short.

## What not to infer

- Do not force every move into every piece.
- Do not add rhetorical questions just to hit the average.
- Do not add bold or lists where the piece reads better as prose.
- Do not copy sample sentences; copy the posture.
