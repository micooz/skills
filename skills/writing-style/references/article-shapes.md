# Article shapes

## Product / open-source note

Use five beats, in order:

1. A concrete personal problem or occasion.
2. What you built, in one or two sentences.
3. How to use it. Keep the steps short.
4. What it looks like now. Use examples, screenshots, or prompts.
5. Limits and links. Do not bury the limits.

Keep the whole piece tight. A product note does not need a history of the category, a maturity model, or a closing slogan.

## Technical / retrospective

Use headings when the piece is long enough to need them. Each section should answer one question. Paragraphs stay short; bullets and tables are allowed when they carry dense information, not when they only decorate.

Strong claims are welcome if the author can defend them. Say "I think" when it is an opinion; do not dress it up as a universal law.

## Opinion / essay

One point per paragraph. A personal image or an unresolved question can close the piece. Do not add uplift just because the piece is ending.

## Short post

One concrete detail, one turn, one question. A few hundred characters is enough. Do not compress a full essay into a thread.

## Length

- Product note: short enough to read in one sitting; keep only the setup, the result, and the limits.
- Technical piece: as long as the mechanism needs, but keep every section focused.
- Opinion piece: shorter than the argument feels; cut the recap.
- Short post: one screen.
